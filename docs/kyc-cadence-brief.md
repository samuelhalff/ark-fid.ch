## Legal frame (do not improvise; prudent reading, labelled as such)
- Ark Fiduciaire SA is affiliated to the SRO SO-FIT; LBA as revised on 1 October 2026.
- Henri Corboz's decision tree (29.10.2026), recorded per client BEFORE anything is sent:
  1. Is the service subject to the LBA? Record the basis: (i) financial intermediary under art. 2 al. 3 LBA (accepting, holding, or helping to invest or transfer third-party assets, e.g. paying bills from client money); (ii) adviser under art. 2 al. 3bis (taking part, for a client, in financial transactions linked to: buying or selling real estate; creating or establishing a non-operating entity seated in Switzerland or any entity seated abroad; managing or administering a non-operating entity; contributions and distributions of such an entity; buying or selling an entity through a non-operating entity); (iii) adviser under art. 2 al. 3ter (providing an address or premises as domicile or registered office for more than six months). Check the exceptions of art. 2 al. 4ter (e.g. let. f: acting as an officer of an operating entity with its seat in Switzerland). An unresolved grey zone is treated as subject (Ark's prudent internal policy). Otherwise "Pas de KYC LBA" with the reason recorded.
  2. Natural person → route `pp` (certified ID).
  3. Legal entity: operating → `pm-op` (art. 2a al. 3: ≥ 25 % alone or in concert → other control → most senior manager); non-operating, in particular domiciliary (art. 2a al. 6) → `pm-nonop` (all beneficial owners of the assets, no threshold); trust / foundation as the client → manual route (no web form).
  4. Supplementary declarations, always outside the web form: a declaration identifying the beneficial owner when an operating company holds money or assets for a third party (flag `forThird`; "declaration A" in Henri's note); a trust or foundation declaration plus a structure chart when a trust or foundation is the client or sits in the ownership chain ("declaration T / S"; flag `trustOrFoundation` for companies). For a natural person who answers `b.ppStructure = yes`, the form only raises `nominee`: open a manual structure review and find out whether a trust or foundation is involved. Sam supplies the approved templates; do not assume the banks' CDB forms A, T or S apply unchanged without SO-FIT confirmation.
  5. PEP and countries: Ark's internal rule (Henri, 29.10.2026; stricter than the law, label it so): any PEP or high-risk country = increased risk and additional measures. By law (financial-intermediary rules, art. 6 al. 3–4 LBA) relationships with foreign PEPs and their close associates always carry increased risk; Swiss or international-organisation PEPs and their close associates only with another risk criterion. Adviser activities follow arts. 8b–8c LBA and the SO-FIT rules, unless art. 2b LBA applies the financial-intermediary rules. Ark's stricter internal rule applies throughout. Country exposure is assessed against dated country lists and the SO-FIT risk method.
- The route is Ark's decision, never the client's.

## What to build
1. **Client LBA file** (per client): LBA qualification (basis, reason), route, risk class (Normal / Accru / Élevé / Inacceptable – refus), responsible, review dates, LBA - OAR folder, relationship start and end dates, history of cases, revisions and decisions.
2. **Triage gate: operating or not** (before any case): a queue of clients without a route (the 22 "À déterminer" and any new client). For each: facts and evidence considered (Zefix purpose, own offices, own employees, accounts, who invoices, role in a group), decision (route or "Pas de KYC LBA" or manual route), rationale, decided by, date, approved by a second person. `POST /cases` creates a live link at once, so the UI must make it impossible before the route and the prefill are approved ("no sending before we check all").
   - How the form handles the status: the route Ark picks decides which section B the client sees (`pm-op`: 25 % cascade; `pm-nonop`: every beneficial owner of the assets, no threshold, with the origin of each one's wealth and an ID copy each). On both company routes the form also asks the client the facts: `a.activityKind` (operates / supports a group / only holds assets / not started), `a.ownPremises`, `a.ownStaff`. Ark can prefill them (shown "à vérifier"); prefill what Ark knows so the client only confirms.
   - Checks on submission: a description that contradicts the route (operating form + "only holds assets"; non-operating form + "operates" or "supports a group") sends the case back to Ark with nothing to sign (`review_route`): staff decide and `reopen` with the right route (the client's answers are kept). An operating form with neither own premises nor own staff raises the flag `noSubstance` (possible domiciliary company): staff confirm or change the route in the review.
   - When Ark cannot decide from what it knows (prudent default): send `pm-nonop`, which collects more (all beneficial owners, no threshold); if the client describes an operating business, the case returns as `review_route` and staff reopen with `pm-op`. Record this default choice in the triage rationale.
3. **Prepare a case**: client, folder, language FR/EN, client e-mail (company: the contact who fills), prefill: company data from Zefix (name, UID, legal form, seat, purpose, incorporation date; people from SOGC only as suggestions to confirm) and what Ark knows (directors, signatories, shareholders, services). Prefill ids = answer ids in the kyc schema. Second-person check, then send.
4. **Send and follow**: `POST /cases` (signed, Idempotency-Key) → link; e-mail the invitation from the Ark mailbox in Sam's voice (FR/EN), mentioning the code e-mail and the documents to prepare. Poll `GET /cases?cursor=` (store the cursor) and show the actionRequired: await_client, review_route (the client's description contradicts the route: decide, then `reopen` with the right route), await_signature, review_kyc. Reminders at D+15 and D+30 if not submitted or not signed (Dima's practice), then escalate to the responsible person. Extra documents (power of attorney, related-company extracts, tax return, items marked "sent separately") are requested by e-mail and tracked per case; staff file them in the case folder under `Compléments/`.
5. **Review = the internal LBA note** (Dima's PM / PP notes, every field): documents received and missing (with reminders), signing powers checked (basis), transparency register (consistent / discrepancy with details and measures / not applicable), company type retained (operating / domiciliary / other non-operating), controlling persons or beneficial owners retained, sanctions, PEP and adverse-media checks (date, tool, result; the client's self-declaration is not a check), plausibility of transactions and amounts, plausibility of source of wealth and funds, licence check, the risk dimensions of the applicable note (PM: country, services, activity, structure, transactions, PEP, sanctions/media, source of wealth/funds; PP: the same without structure), overall class with reasons, additional measures (required for Accru / Élevé), prepared by / validated by with dates (four eyes: the validator is never the preparer and has no personal interest in the client, e.g. Sam as co-investor), review frequency and next review.
   - Country risk: record the list used (e.g. FATF high-risk and increased-monitoring lists, with their date), the countries checked (nationality, domicile, seat, tax country, beneficial owners) and the result.
   - Acceptance of the signed scan before validation: every page carries the reference of the revision under review (`<caseId>-R<n>-<sha8>`), all pages present, signed by persons whose signing powers were checked, legible; certified ID copies received (original by post when required); supplementary declarations A / T / S received when flagged.
   - Files: read them with Graph through Cadence's own app registration (Sites.Selected, read on LBA - OAR; ask Sam to create it), using the driveId and item ids of the case summary (`latestRevision.json`, `pdf`, `signed[]`, `documents[].files[]`). Check the SHA-256 shown in the summary before relying on `latestRevision.json`, `signed[]` or `documents[].files[]`. The API exposes no SHA-256 for the generated `pdf`: identify it by item id, name and `declarationId`.
   - Output: generate the note as a document (PDF) into `KYC <caseId>/Révision <n>/`, then update the register (StatutKYC "Contrôlé et validé", Risque, DerniereRevue, ProchaineRevue).
6. **Refusal and suspicion** (prudent reading): risk "Inacceptable – refus" is decided by Sam (or the LBA responsible person) with reasons; Cadence then blocks any new case or link for that client and marks the register. If facts may indicate money laundering or terrorist financing, Cadence sends nothing to the client and escalates immediately to the LBA responsible person. That person promptly determines whether the conditions of art. 9 LBA are met; if they are, Ark must report to MROS immediately and follow the SO-FIT MROS directive. Neither the persons concerned nor third parties may be informed of the report, subject to the exceptions in art. 10a LBA. Documents relating to reports and MROS information requests go into a separate restricted file (art. 34 LBA), with disclosure limited under al. 2, and are destroyed five years after the report (al. 4): Cadence schedules that deletion for approval in time. Ordinary KYC records stay under art. 7.
7. **Corrections and changes**: `reopen` (expectedRevision) with corrected prefill creates a new revision and a new PDF to sign. A change of beneficial owner, controlling person, shareholder, director, signatory or activity reported by the client or found by Ark opens a change event in Cadence; a change of beneficial owner always triggers renewed identification and documentation and, as Ark's internal policy, a new signed declaration (reopen → new revision → new signed PDF; art. 4 al. 2 LBA requires the declaration in writing or in another form evidenced by text). Other changes are assessed by the responsible person.
8. **Periodic reviews**: frequency set in the note per risk class and the SO-FIT regulation, at least annual for Accru and Élevé (Dima's note; Sam confirms the Normal-risk interval). Due and overdue reviews in a queue with escalation. A review re-uses the last answers as prefill: `reopen` on an open file, or a new case when the previous case was closed (`close` is irreversible and means "this case is finished", not "reviewed").
9. **Retention** (except the art. 34 records of step 6): record the end of the relationship (or of the transaction); documents may be deleted at the earliest ten years later (art. 7 LBA is a minimum), subject to any longer requirement and to a legal hold. Nothing is deleted automatically: deletion is listed for Sam's approval, logged, and blocked by a legal hold. Abandoned drafts (never submitted) are kept with the case until Sam decides otherwise.
