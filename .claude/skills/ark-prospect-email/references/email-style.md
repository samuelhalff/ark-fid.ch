# Email style — Ark Fiduciaire prospect replies

Calibrated on Samuel's own emails. Update this whenever he corrects a draft.

## Reference email (Samuel's, real, EN — incorporation enquiry)

```
Dear Zhaoyu,

Thanks for reaching out.

We would be happy to assist you in creating a company in Switzerland.

Our fees will vary depending on the activity and scope (incorporation – company
form AG or GmbH, accounting, VAT, taxes, directorship, etc).

Would you have a moment to talk over the phone this week, or via Teams?

We can assist in the incorporation of the company. Notary fees will depend on the
form of the company.

You will probably also need a director based in Switzerland (Swiss law
requirement), which we can also provide.

Kindly let me know what day/time would be convenient for a call.

Kind regards,

Samuel
```

What to copy from it: one sentence per paragraph, blank line between paragraphs,
no figures, no bullet lists, no marketing language, plain "Dear <first name>",
sign-off "Kind regards," then "Samuel".

## Shape

```
Subject: <short, concrete, in the prospect's language — their topic, not "Your enquiry">

Dear <first name>,

<Thank them for reaching out via our website.>

<We would be happy to assist with <what they asked for>.>

<One or two concrete Swiss-specific points that show we understood the case —
 a legal requirement, a step they will need, something they did not ask about
 but will hit. This is where the value is.>

<Fees: "Our fees will vary depending on the activity and scope (…)" — list the
 likely scope items in brackets. Figures only when the case is clean and covered
 by quote-instructions.md, and then only as a range with the VAT note.>

<Ask for a 15-minute Teams call at their convenience in the coming days, and ask
 them to name a day/time.>

Kind regards,

Samuel
```

## Rules of thumb

- **One sentence per paragraph.** Blank line between each. This is the house rhythm.
- **Length:** 5–10 short paragraphs. If it needs more, it needs a call instead.
- **No figures in a first email** unless the case is clean, the service is covered
  by `quote-instructions.md`, and Samuel hasn't said otherwise. Default is
  "our fees will vary depending on the activity and scope (…)".
- **Always include one or two substantive points** — the Swiss-resident director
  requirement, notary fees depending on company form, VAT registration thresholds,
  source tax, whatever their case actually implies. Never a generic "we offer a
  full range of services".
- **No service catalogue.** Only what they asked about, plus what their case forces.
- **No marketing adjectives** — no "leading", "bespoke", "we are thrilled".
- **No bullet lists** unless the prospect asked several distinct questions.
- Scope items go in brackets after the fee sentence, comma-separated, like the
  reference email.
- Plain sentences. Vouvoiement in French.
- **Never** name a lead source, referral entity, or another fiduciary brand.
- **Never** explain how our pricing is built.

## Openers (always thank them for the website contact)

- EN: "Thank you for reaching out to us through our website."
- FR: « Merci d'avoir pris contact avec nous via notre site. »
- DE: „Vielen Dank für Ihre Anfrage über unsere Website."
- IT: «Grazie per averci contattato tramite il nostro sito.»

## Call to action

Ask for the call, then ask them to name the time. Two sentences, like the reference
email — not one.

- EN: "Would you have 15 minutes for a Teams call in the coming days?" /
  "Kindly let me know what day/time would be convenient."
- FR: « Auriez-vous 15 minutes pour un échange Teams dans les prochains jours ? » /
  « Dites-moi simplement quel jour et quelle heure vous conviendraient. »
- DE: „Hätten Sie in den nächsten Tagen 15 Minuten Zeit für ein Teams-Gespräch?" /
  „Sagen Sie mir gerne, welcher Tag und welche Uhrzeit Ihnen passen."
- IT: «Avrebbe 15 minuti per una call Teams nei prossimi giorni?» /
  «Mi faccia sapere quale giorno e orario le sarebbero comodi.»

Do not propose specific slots unless Samuel asks for it. A phone call can be offered
as an alternative to Teams, as in the reference email.

The booking link (see `company-facts.md`) is **optional** — add it only when Samuel
asks, or when the thread has gone back and forth without a time being fixed. Default
is to let them name the time.

## If the email does contain figures

- Ranges only, never a single figure (except the fixed hourly rates).
- Monthly first, annual equivalent in parentheses, where applicable.
- Close with the VAT note in the prospect's language:
  - EN: Prices excl. VAT; final scope and KYC/complexity review may adjust the final quote.
  - FR: Prix hors TVA; le périmètre final et la revue KYC/complexité peuvent ajuster le devis.
  - DE: Preise exkl. MwSt.; endgültiger Umfang und KYC-/Komplexitätsprüfung können das Angebot anpassen.
  - IT: Prezzi IVA esclusa; perimetro finale e revisione KYC/complessità possono adeguare il preventivo.
- No summary table in a first email. Two service lines at most.

## Sign-off

```
Kind regards,

Samuel
```

- FR: « Bien à vous, » / DE: „Freundliche Grüsse," / IT: «Cordiali saluti,»

First name only. Outlook appends the full signature block — do **not** type it into
the body when sending through Outlook. When sending through the Graph script, no
signature is appended automatically, so append the block from `company-facts.md`.

## Language

Reply in the language the prospect wrote in. Never mix languages within one email.
Swiss spelling in German (ss, never ß).
