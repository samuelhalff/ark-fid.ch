# Email style — Ark Fiduciaire prospect replies

Working draft. Samuel refines this over time; update it whenever he corrects a draft.

## Shape

```
Subject: <short, concrete, in the prospect's language — their topic, not "Your enquiry">

<Greeting>,

<Thank them for reaching out via our website.>

<One or two lines showing we understood what they need — restate their situation, not ours.>

<One or two lines: what we'd do / what we need to know to be precise. Fees only if
 references/quote-instructions.md covers the case.>

<Offer a 15-minute Teams call at their convenience in the coming days.>

<Sign-off>
<Signature block>
```

## Rules of thumb

- **Length:** 5–10 lines of body. If it needs more, it needs a call instead.
- **One ask per email.** The ask is the 15-minute call.
- **No service catalogue.** Mention only the services they actually asked about.
- **No marketing adjectives** — no "leading", "bespoke", "cutting-edge", "we are thrilled".
- **No pricing hedges.** Either a figure from the quote instructions, or a call to scope it.
- **Plain sentences.** Professional, direct, Geneva-fiduciary register. Vouvoiement in French.
- **No em dashes, no bullet lists** unless the prospect listed several distinct questions.
- **Never** name a lead source, referral entity, or another fiduciary brand.

## Openers (always thank them for the website contact)

- FR: « Merci d'avoir pris contact avec nous via notre site. »
- EN: "Thank you for reaching out to us through our website."
- DE: „Vielen Dank für Ihre Anfrage über unsere Website."
- IT: «Grazie per averci contattato tramite il nostro sito.»

## Call to action

- FR: « Seriez-vous disponible pour un appel Teams de 15 minutes dans les prochains jours ? Dites-moi simplement ce qui vous arrange. »
- EN: "Would you have 15 minutes for a Teams call in the coming days? Just tell me what suits you."
- DE: „Hätten Sie in den nächsten Tagen 15 Minuten Zeit für ein Teams-Gespräch? Sagen Sie mir einfach, was Ihnen passt."
- IT: «Avrebbe 15 minuti per una call Teams nei prossimi giorni? Mi dica pure cosa le è più comodo.»

Do not propose specific slots unless Samuel asks for it. Let them name the time.

## Sign-offs

- FR: « Bien à vous, » / EN: "Kind regards," / DE: „Freundliche Grüsse," / IT: «Cordiali saluti,»

## Language

Reply in the language the prospect wrote in. Never mix languages within one email.
Swiss spelling in German (ss, never ß).
