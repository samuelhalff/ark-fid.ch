Apply matter by matter, when the file is opened and whenever its purpose changes. The type of service (opinion, call, contract review) decides nothing; only the underlying transaction counts.

New matter

  1. Is the matter contentious?

    Art. 2 para. 4 AMLA

    • Judicial, administrative, criminal or arbitral proceedings
    • Advice to prepare, conduct or avoid proceedings, assessment of litigation risk
    • Enforcement of the outcome of proceedings

    If no: next step

    If yesOutside AMLA

  2. Does the matter involve a listed transaction?

    Art. 2 paras. 3bis and 3ter AMLA

    • Purchase or sale of real estate
    • Formation of a NOE in Switzerland, or of any entity seated abroad
    • Management or administration of a NOE
    • Contributions to and distributions by a NOE
    • Purchase or sale of an entity through a NOE
    • Domiciliation of an entity at the firm for more than 6 months

    If noOutside AMLA

    Employment contract, commercial lease, distribution, T&C, NDA, general tax advice, formation of a Swiss operating company, share deal between operating companies.

  3. Does your advice causally contribute to the transaction?

    Art. 12d AMLO. Phone calls and written advice are treated the same.

    Outside AMLAGeneral information on the law, without reviewing the client's situation
    Grey zoneRecommendation tailored to the client, who then acts on it

    If yes or Grey zone: next step

    Grey zoneIn dubio pro AMLA

    Identify the client as soon as the discussion becomes specific, not when the memo is delivered.

  4. Is there a financial transaction?

    Art. 2 para. 3bis AMLA, including the organisation of funds; art. 12e AMLO for real estate

    • Sale price, capital payment, contribution in kind, distribution, loan
    • Real estate: as soon as the parties have declared their intention to conclude the sale
    • Cash Any cash payment in a real estate transaction is now subject to due diligence, whatever the amount

    If yes: next step

    If noOutside AMLA

    Rare in practice for listed transactions.

  5. Does an exception apply?

    Art. 2 para. 4ter AMLA; AMLO for intra-group

    • Transfer below CHF 5 million, price paid and received exclusively through a bank or a regulated FI. A cash payment defeats the exception.
    • Residential property for own use or as a replacement
    • Family and inheritance law; foundation upon death
    • Officer of a Swiss operating entity
    • Notarisation without ancillary advice; intra-group services

    If no: next step

    If yesOutside AMLA for this matter

    The internal AMLA unit certifies the exception in writing.

  6. Is the professional basis threshold met?

    Art. 12f AMLO. For a firm organised as a legal entity, the revenue and number thresholds are probably assessed at firm level.

    • More than CHF 50,000 of AMLA gross revenue per calendar year
    • More than 20 clients or 20 transactions per calendar year
    • More than CHF 5 million of third-party assets at any one time
    • More than CHF 2 million of transactions per calendar year

    If yes: result below

    If noNot yet subject

    Keep the register of AMLA matters. Once a threshold is crossed: two months to join an SRO, and no new AMLA client before joining.

Advisor under the AMLA

  • Join the SRO SAV/SNV, the only SRO currently accepting lawyers as advisors according to the Bar, before 1 December 2026. Collective membership of the firm recommended.
  • No AMLA matter from a new client before membership takes effect. Ongoing matters and existing clients: allowed if the application is filed in time.
  • The lawyer responsible for the matter is a member or registered. Associates, trainees and paralegals are auxiliaries: written agreement, training, billing by the advisor.
  • No report to MROS as long as the lawyer only advises (art. 9 para. 2 AMLA).

Advisor under the AMLA

  • Fiduciary already a member as FI: notify the advisor activity to its SRO before 1 December 2026 (art. 12a AMLA), after checking that it registers advisors.
  • Otherwise: application to join an SRO within the same deadline.
  • From 1 October 2026: identification of the client and beneficial owner, purpose of the transaction, documentation (arts. 8b and 8c AMLA), internal organisation (art. 8d AMLA).
  • Record the matter in the register of AMLA matters and assess its risk.

The duty lies with the client company

Filing with the register is the company's own duty, through the most senior member of its management body. That person may delegate the task but remains responsible. Neither the law firm nor the fiduciary has a filing duty of its own.

Major exception: where you sit as director or manager of a client company, the duty is yours.

But clients should be advised

No law requires you to inform clients. It is nonetheless prudent:

  • many companies take 1 October 2026 as the deadline, when it is the start of the deadlines;
  • an inaccurate entry will be flagged by the bank, which must report discrepancies to the register;
  • identifying the BO, preparing the documents and filing on EasyGov are a service to offer.

Key facts

Who is a BO

The natural person holding at least 25% of the capital or votes, or otherwise controlling the company. The definition is that of the AMLA, not of the old company law.

Duties of BOs

Report their status, and any change, within one month. Where control runs through a chain, the report goes directly to the company.

Filing

Online on EasyGov, free of charge. Any later change must be filed within one month.

The register

Kept by the Federal Office of Justice. Not public and declaratory. Each company can obtain an extract of its own data.

Penalty

Fine of up to CHF 500,000 for breach of the filing duties.

Record keeping

Supporting documents kept for ten years after the person ceases to be a BO. The list kept under the old law is kept until 1 October 2036.

For you

Advisors and FIs may consult the register for their due diligence.

An FI that finds a discrepancy with its own information must report it to the register if doubts remain after raising it with the client.

The register does not replace your own identification of the BO (art. 4 AMLA).

Real estate Cash

Another change on 1 October 2026: in real estate transactions, every cash payment is subject to AMLA due diligence, whatever the amount.

Concepts that tip a matter

With no practice or case law yet, these are where the risk of error lies.

Non-operational

Entity not formed or run to operate or support the operating business of an enterprise. Abbreviated NOE.

Covered: personal holding company, passive holding company for real estate or securities, domiciliary company. Board mandates of such entities, however, fall under the existing FI regime. In principle not covered: a genuine holding company of an operating group. Mixed cases (SME holding that also accumulates cash): in dubio pro AMLA.

Abroad

Formation of any entity abroad is covered, whether operating or not. The NOE test applies only to Swiss entities.

Causality

A contribution without which the transaction would not take place. Advice alone is enough: you need not handle the funds.

The form is irrelevant (call, email, memo). What counts: an identified client, a concrete transaction, advice the client acts on.

Financial transaction

Movement of assets linked to the transaction: price, capital payment, contribution, distribution, loan. The organisation of funds is included.

Professional basis

Any one of the four thresholds is enough: CHF 50,000 of revenue, 20 clients or transactions, CHF 5 million of assets, CHF 2 million of transactions. The activity can be professional even below the thresholds.

A single Geneva property sale often crosses the CHF 2 million threshold on its own.

6 months

Duration threshold for domiciliation (art. 2 para. 3ter AMLA). Beyond it, providing an address or premises is covered, whatever the entity.
